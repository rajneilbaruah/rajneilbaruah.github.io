01 · About

A physicist interested in the hard parts of inference.

I am a Ph.D. researcher in particle physics at Bennett University, advised by Dr. Subhadeep Mondal. I also work as a Junior Research Fellow on the DST–SERB CRG project “A Deep Analysis Framework for Physics Beyond the Standard Model.” under Dr. Sunando Kumar Patra at Bangabasi Evening College, University of Calcutta.

My broad goal is simple: find better ways of learning about elementary particles from data. That includes studying possible physics beyond the Standard Model, building machine-learning methods that can recognise useful patterns in collider events, and developing statistical tools that can search large spaces of possible explanations without becoming prohibitively expensive.

My work sits between physics, statistics, and computation. I am particularly interested in methods that are not only accurate, but also practical: faster to run, lighter to deploy, and easier to use in real scientific workflows.

In plain language

So, what do I actually do?

01

Look for new physics

Our current theory of fundamental particles works remarkably well, but it is not the whole story. I study theoretical models that could explain what the Standard Model leaves unanswered and ask how present or future collider data can test them.

02

Teach computers to spot patterns

Particle collisions can produce extremely complicated sprays of particles called jets. I develop machine-learning methods that learn subtle patterns in these jets so that interesting events can be identified more efficiently.

03

Make hard searches faster

Testing a model can involve exploring thousands or millions of possible parameter choices. I work on statistical and generative methods that can make that exploration much more efficient.

02 · Research

Three connected research directions.

Different tools, one broad question: how can we extract more reliable information from difficult particle-physics data?

01

Inference in difficult parameter spaces

I develop ML-assisted methods for Bayesian inference, including normalizing-flow-based approaches that help conventional sampling methods explore complicated, high-dimensional, or multi-modal distributions.

Normalizing FlowsNested SamplingMCMC
02

Machine learning for collider signatures

I design jet-tagging methods using image-based, graph-based, and attention-based representations, with particular attention to lightweight and computationally efficient architectures.

CNNsGNNsTransformers
03

BSM phenomenology

I study scenarios beyond the Standard Model, including non-minimal Higgs sectors, seesaw models, and R-parity-violating supersymmetry, using collider observables to constrain their parameter spaces.
