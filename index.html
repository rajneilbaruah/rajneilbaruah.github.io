About Me

I am a Ph.D. scholar in Particle Physics at Bennett University and a Junior Research Fellow at Bangabasi Evening College, University of Calcutta. My research focuses on developing machine-learning-driven statistical frameworks to probe complex Beyond the Standard Model theories.

My work spans simulation-based inference, normalizing flows, jet tagging, and efficient deep learning architectures for collider phenomenology.

Research

  • Simulation-Based Inference using Normalizing Flows and Nested Sampling
  • Machine-learning-based jet tagging using GNNs and lightweight CNNs
  • Global parameter inference in BSM models
  • Generative models and diffusion methods for scientific inference
