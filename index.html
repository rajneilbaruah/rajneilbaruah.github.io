About Me

I am a Ph.D. scholar in Physics at Bennett University (2022–present), advised by Dr. Subhadeep Mondal. I am also a Junior Research Fellow at Bangabasi Evening College, University of Calcutta, working under the DST–SERB CRG project “A Deep Analysis Framework for Physics Beyond the Standard Model” (CRG/2022/003208).

My research focuses on the phenomenology of Beyond the Standard Model (BSM) scenarios, including non-minimal Higgs sectors, Seesaw models, and R-parity-violating supersymmetry. A central theme of my work is developing statistically robust and computationally efficient tools to constrain high-dimensional BSM parameter spaces using collider data.

I received my M.Sc. in Physics from the University of Delhi (2020), where my dissertation focused on non-perturbative methods in quantum field theory, and a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Physics from the University of Delhi (2018).

Research

  • Simulation-Based Inference & Normalizing Flows: Developing hybrid inference frameworks that combine normalizing flows with Nested Sampling and MCMC to efficiently explore multi-modal, high-dimensional BSM parameter spaces.
  • Machine Learning for Jet Tagging: Designing and comparing GNN-, CNN-, and attention-based jet taggers to identify boosted objects at hadron colliders, with a focus on lightweight and computationally efficient architectures.
  • Global Fits of BSM Models: Applying ML-assisted inference to constrain models such as Type-II Seesaw and RPV-MSSM using collider observables.
  • Generative & Amortized Inference: Exploring likelihood-weighted normalizing flows, diffusion models, and other generative approaches for rapid posterior estimation.
